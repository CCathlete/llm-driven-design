WHY THE OUTCOMES CHANGED — THIS IS EXPECTED, NOT A DEFECT
Under the old Bayesian probe-count score five passing probes reached ~0.857
purely by being counted, regardless of which coordinates they covered. Under
the new coverage-and-falsification model, prior=0.5 with only 1-2 declared
coordinates and positional fallback gives coverage ~= 0.5, score ~= 0.725,
which is correctly below the 0.85 gate. The decision changing from GREEN to
RED is the feature working as designed — it is the probe-count inflation this
feature exists to remove. Backward compatibility of those decision outcomes
is explicitly not a requirement. The fix therefore keeps GREEN by giving the
fixtures explicit, complete coordinate mappings (five passes per coordinate
=> coverage 0.96875 => score 0.936 >= 0.85): GREEN is then earned under the
new rules rather than inherited from the old ones. No production code is bent
to reproduce the old result.
